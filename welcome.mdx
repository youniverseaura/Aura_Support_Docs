## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Aura free to try?">
    Yes. The Free plan includes chat with live search and voice mode.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my data private?">
    Chat routes use zero data retention, so nothing is kept or used for training.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I reach a person?">
    Email support@youniverse.ai, 24/7.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I choose between Work Mode, Agents and AI Builder?">
    Use [Work Mode](/chat/work-mode) for an action now in connected apps, [Agents](/build/agents) for recurring jobs, and [AI Builder](/build/ai-app-builder) for an app people can use. Each has separate access and usage rules.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
