Big jobs can be split across a **team**: a lead Agent plus helper Agents that each own part of the work.

## How teams work

- A **team channel** appears once a lead has at least one helper. It shows your posts and team updates.
- Each Agent keeps its own **direct messages** for private chat and detailed work.
- Every message has one home — nothing is duplicated between the channel and DMs.
- Events a helper receives wake the lead, who routes the work.

## Shared resources

The whole team can use your shared Vault items, servers, browser session and every project knowledge base — always limited to your own account.

## Example

A "Weekly growth report" lead asks one helper to pull ad metrics, another to summarize support tickets, then combines both into one report and posts it in the channel.
