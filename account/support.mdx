## Our guarantee

If a bug on our side isn't fixed within 24 hours of your report, your next month is free. Outages at third-party providers are excluded.

## Before you write

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Something looks out of date">Hard-refresh the page (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows).</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A connected app stopped working">Open Settings → Connected apps and reconnect it.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm out of credits">Check your balance under the composer and your plan under Settings → Billing.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Include your chat ID">Each chat shows a short ID like TX-1A2B3C under the composer. Sharing it helps us find the problem fast.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What information helps support investigate a problem?">
    Include what you tried, what you expected and the chat's short ID when available. Check the [status page](https://chat.youniverse.ai/status) for a service incident. Do not include passwords or private API keys in your report.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Check live service health at [aura.youniverse.ai/status](https://aura.youniverse.ai/status).
