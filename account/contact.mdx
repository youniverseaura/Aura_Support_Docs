## Human support

Human support is available once you [sign up for a free Aura account](https://aura.youniverse.ai/signin). No paid subscription is required. After signing in, use the support chat inside Aura or email [support@youniverse.ai](mailto:support@youniverse.ai).

For support information and our service guarantee, see [Get help](/account/support).
