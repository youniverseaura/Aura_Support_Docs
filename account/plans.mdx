Credits pay for heavier work: advanced chat modes, agent runs, media generation and app building. Your remaining balance shows under the composer.

See current plan prices on the [Aura home page](https://aura.youniverse.ai) — tap **Pricing** at the top.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What do the plans cost?">
    Essential is $4/month with 200 credits, Pro is $30/month with 1,500 credits and Elite is $90/month with 5,000 credits. Free costs nothing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do unused credits roll over?">
    Monthly plan credits reset each billing cycle. Top-up credits you buy never expire.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I buy more credits?">
    Yes. Essential, Pro and Elite members can buy top-up packs any time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change plans later?">
    Yes. Upgrades start right away and you only pay the difference for the rest of the month. Downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
