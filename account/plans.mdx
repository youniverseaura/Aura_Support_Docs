## Credits

<Frame>
  <img
    src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/plans-tiers.jpg"
    alt="Aura plan tiers and credits"
  />
</Frame>

Credits pay for heavier work: advanced chat modes, agent runs, media generation, app building and hosting. Your remaining balance shows under the composer.

Chat image generation is available on every paid tier, including Essential. It is separate from AI Studio, whose generation tools require Pro or Elite. Paid image generation uses credits and remains subject to usage limits; it is not unlimited.

Free chat allows 20 messages per rolling hour with no daily cap, plus 2 images per rolling two hours. Essential includes Work Mode in Quick Chat; projects, knowledge bases and Agents require Pro or Elite.

The prices above are current standard USD prices; existing subscriptions and discounts may differ. The current Elite plan includes 5,000 monthly credits. Eligible legacy Elite subscriptions retain their recorded allowance, including 3,000 monthly credits on the older \$59.99 plan. Eligible legacy accounts may also retain Builder access.

See current plan prices on the [Aura home page](https://aura.youniverse.ai) — tap **Pricing** at the top.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What do the plans cost?">
    Essential is \$7.99/month with 200 credits, Pro is \$29.99/month with 1,500 credits and Elite is \$90/month with 5,000 credits. Free costs nothing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do unused credits roll over?">
    Monthly plan credits reset each billing cycle. Top-up credits you buy never expire.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I buy more credits?">
    Yes. Essential, Pro and Elite members can buy top-up packs any time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change plans later?">
    Yes. Upgrades start right away and you only pay the difference for the rest of the month. Downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does my subscription make every tool task free?">
    No. A plan unlocks access and includes its monthly credit allowance; heavier work such as agent runs, media generation and app builds still uses credits. [Work Mode costs](/work-mode/privacy-and-credits) depend on the actual task, not just the plan name.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
