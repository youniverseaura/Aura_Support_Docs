## How credits work

<Frame>
  <img
    src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/plans-tiers.jpg"
    alt="Aura plan tiers and credits"
  />
</Frame>

Credits pay for heavier work: advanced chat modes, agent runs, media generation and app building. Your remaining balance always shows under the composer, so you're never guessing.

- **Monthly credits** come with your plan and reset each billing cycle.
- **Top-up credits** can be bought any time by Essential, Pro and Elite members, and never expire.
- **Upgrades** start right away and you only pay the difference for the rest of the month. **Downgrades** take effect at the end of your billing period.

## How billing works

- **Payments are processed by Stripe**, the same payment platform used by millions of businesses. Aura never sees or stores your card number — it goes straight to Stripe over an encrypted connection.
- **Manage everything yourself.** Update your card, change plans, download invoices or cancel from Settings → Billing, through the secure Stripe customer portal.
- **Cancel any time.** Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for, and you keep your remaining top-up credits.
- **Builder hosting reserve.** Published AI Builder apps hold a 30-day hosting reserve with automatic top-up, so a busy app never goes dark. You'll get a warning 3 days before anything pauses. See [Storage & hosting](/builder/storage).
