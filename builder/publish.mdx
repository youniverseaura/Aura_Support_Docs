## Auto Publish

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/autopublish.jpg" alt="Glass night clock next to a rocket on a launch pad" />
</Frame>

Once a project has been published, **Auto Publish** is on by default: it ships your latest successful changes once a day at 2:01 AM New York time. Use the switch next to Publish to turn it off or choose **Instantly**, **Every hour** or **Every 6 hours**. It's one shared setting for everyone on the project.

<Note>Live apps keep running — and using hosting credits — after you close the browser. Hosting is usage-based; see **Settings → Usage & billing**.</Note>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When does Auto Publish run?">
    Once a day at 2:01 AM New York time by default. You can switch it to instantly, hourly, every 6 hours, or off.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does my app cost anything after I close the browser?">
    Live apps keep running and use hosting credits based on usage.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is publishing free?">
    Yes, publishing itself is always free.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do API-key changes require another AI build?">
    Not just to make an existing live key change take effect: a republish is what updates the live environment. Aura starts it automatically when there are no unpublished code changes; otherwise it asks first. If the AI is waiting for requested keys, saving the last one can separately start a paid continuation. See [Setup & keys](/builder/setup-keys).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does publishing a key also publish my other changes?">
    Publish deploys the current app version, not just an individual key. That is why Aura asks before republishing a key update when preview contains unpublished work. Review the preview before choosing to publish.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
