Click **Publish** to deploy the current version to your live address. Progress streams in the **Logs** tab. Publishing is always free.

## Auto Publish

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/autopublish.jpg" alt="Glass night clock next to a rocket on a launch pad" />
</Frame>

Once a project has been published, **Auto Publish** is on by default: it ships your latest successful changes once a day at 2:01 AM New York time. Use the switch next to Publish to turn it off or choose **Instantly**, **Every hour** or **Every 6 hours**. It's one shared setting for everyone on the project.

<Note>Live apps keep running — and using hosting credits — after you close the browser. Hosting is usage-based; see **Settings → Usage & billing**.</Note>
