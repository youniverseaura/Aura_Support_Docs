The moment your app is live on its own domain, Aura sets up Google for you — nothing to connect:

- Verifies you own the domain with Google
- Registers it in **Search Console** and submits your sitemap
- Creates a **Google Analytics** property and adds the tag
- Sends crawl hints so search engines notice new pages faster

## The SEO tab

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/analytics.jpg" alt="Glass dashboard with a rising chart, globe and calendar" />
</Frame>

Updated daily:

- **Visitors**, Google clicks, appearances and average position
- **Top pages** and **top search terms**
- **Google PageSpeed** on phone and computer, with Core Web Vitals
- **Growth opportunities** from Semrush: keywords, competitors and quick wins

New sites can take a few days to show numbers.

## Ask in plain words

Quick checks you can run anytime, free: **Am I on Google?**, **What are people searching?**, **Submit my sitemap**, **How's my traffic?** and **Check my page titles**.

## Common questions
