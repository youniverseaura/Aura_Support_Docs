Every app comes with its own database, file storage and hosting — nothing to set up.

## How storage works

Your app's storage is two things added together:

- **Code and files** — your app's pages, images, uploads and other assets.
- **Database** — every record your app saves (users, orders, posts, bookings).

Each project can grow to **10 TB**. See your usage in **Settings → Storage**; checking it is free and never wakes a sleeping app.

## Hosting

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/builder/builder-hosting.jpg" alt="Glass server cloud with a rising traffic line and a battery topped up with coins" />
</Frame>

Live apps keep running after you close your browser. Hosting is usage-based — storage is billed monthly per GB, plus traffic.

- **Hosting reserve:** each live app holds about 30 days of hosting in reserve, so it never goes dark unexpectedly.
- **Auto top-up:** keeps a live app funded on its own. Find it in **Settings → Usage & billing**.
- **Spike watch:** Aura watches for sudden traffic spikes and warns you early.

## Credits

| | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| First build of a new app | Actual build-service usage; minimum balance of 500 credits to start, not a flat fee |
| A small edit | About 180 credits |
| Asking questions in builder chat | Free |
| Publishing | Free |
| Editing data, viewing logs and history | Free |
| Visual edits made by hand | Free |
| Restoring a version or adding a domain | Credits |

You see a live credit counter while a build runs. Cancelled runs bill only what they used, and a nightly audit refunds any overcharge — it never charges more. If you run out mid-build, the run pauses and resumes when you top up.

**Settings → Usage & billing** breaks spending down by builds, in-app AI and hosting.
