Describe the game you've always wanted — a racer, a puzzle, an arcade shooter — and the AI Builder creates a playable 3D game in the browser.

## A good first prompt

> Build a 3D arcade racing game with three tracks, a lap timer, a leaderboard and touch controls for phones.

Then refine: add power-ups, tune the camera, change the art style.
