Most builders stop at "here's your app." Aura keeps going: it measures your live app with **Google PageSpeed**, studies your market with **Semrush**, and hands both to **Gemini 4 Argon**, which turns them into one page-by-page plan the AI Builder can carry out with a single tap.

## The three parts

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Google PageSpeed" icon="gauge">
    Google's own Lighthouse test, on phone and computer: speed, SEO, accessibility and best-practice scores, Core Web Vitals and a fix list.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Semrush" icon="chart-line">
    What people actually search for: your keywords, estimated monthly visits, authority score, linking sites, competitors, keyword gaps and quick wins.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Gemini 4 Argon" icon="sparkles">
    Reads your live pages, the PageSpeed results and the Semrush data together, and writes one prioritized, page-specific plan.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Why the combo matters

Each tool alone tells you half the story. PageSpeed says *what's slow* but not what to write. Semrush says *what people search* but not what's broken on your pages. Static checks say *what's missing* but not what matters most. Gemini 4 Argon connects them — so the plan says, for example, "compress the hero image on your home page, and add a section answering the question your competitors rank for."

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Measure">
    Aura checks every page (titles, descriptions, headings, content length, sitemap, robots, icons, share images) and runs Google PageSpeed on phone and computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Research">
    Semrush adds your keywords, competitors and quick wins — searches you nearly rank for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan">
    Gemini 4 Argon (medium reasoning) writes one prioritized plan, page by page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix">
    Tap **Fix** and the AI Builder applies the plan in one build.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-measure">
    After the build, PageSpeed runs again. If phone or computer is still under 90, Aura offers a second speed round (up to two).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need my own Semrush or Google account?">
    No. Aura provides PageSpeed, Semrush data and Google Search Console setup for you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Semrush numbers?">
    Semrush data appears once your app is live on its own domain. New sites can take a few days to show up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many speed rounds will it run?">
    Up to two. After each fix, PageSpeed re-measures; a second round is offered only if a score is still under 90.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are Semrush traffic estimates the same as my actual visitors?">
    No. Semrush provides estimates about organic search visibility and competitors. Google Analytics and Search Console report different measured activity. Compare like-for-like metrics rather than treating estimated search visits as your full visitor count. See [SEO and analytics](/builder/seo).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
