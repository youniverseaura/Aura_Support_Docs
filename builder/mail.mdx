Once your domain uses Aura's nameservers, open **Domain Hub → Mail** to create real mailboxes, aliases and forwarding — and give your app a sending address.

## Set up for you

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/envelope-trust.jpg" alt="Glass envelope sealed with a check-mark shield" />
</Frame>

Aura publishes the records that keep your mail out of spam folders and writes your app's sending settings into the project. They show as **Set up by Aura** in Setup & keys — you never need to find a key.

<Warning>Mail needs nameserver delegation. If you only point records at Aura, mail stays pending and nothing can be sent.</Warning>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is my mail stuck on pending?">
    Mail needs your domain to use Aura's nameservers. Pointing records alone isn't enough.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to find an email key?">
    No. Aura writes the sending settings for you; they show as Set up by Aura.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
