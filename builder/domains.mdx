<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Buy" icon="shopping-cart">Search and buy with credits. Your first domain on a project is free.</Card>
  <Card title="Transfer" icon="arrow-right-left">Move one in with its EPP code. You're charged only when the transfer completes.</Card>
  <Card title="Connect" icon="link">Keep it at your registrar and add the records shown on the live-checking setup card.</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Managed DNS and backups

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/dns.jpg" alt="Servers connected to a house, shield and backup box" />
</Frame>

Domains bought or transferred into Aura are managed for you: records stay correct and certificates are issued automatically. Aura saves a backup before any change and one every month, keeping 12 months of history under **Domain Hub → DNS backups**.
