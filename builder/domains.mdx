## Managed DNS and backups

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/dns.jpg" alt="Servers connected to a house, shield and backup box" />
</Frame>

Domains bought or transferred into Aura are managed for you: records stay correct and certificates are issued automatically. Aura saves a backup before any change and one every month, keeping 12 months of history under **Domain Hub → DNS backups**.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is my first domain free?">
    Yes, your first domain on each project is free.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When am I charged for a transfer?">
    Only when the transfer completes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are DNS changes backed up?">
    Yes. A backup before every change plus a monthly one, with 12 months kept.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to transfer my existing domain to Aura?">
    No. **Connect** keeps the domain at your registrar and shows the DNS records to add. **Transfer** moves its registration using an EPP code. If you want domain mail, also review the [nameserver requirements](/builder/mail).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
