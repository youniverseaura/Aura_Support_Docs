Spreadsheets work until five people edit them at once. An internal dashboard gives your team one live source of truth.

![A glass analytics dashboard](/images/use-cases/dashboard.jpg)

## What to ask for

> Build an internal dashboard for my sales team. Sign-in for each rep, a home page with this week's numbers as charts, a page to log new deals, and a leaderboard. Only signed-in team members can see it.

The Builder creates the database, the charts, the forms and the sign-in — a real internal tool, not a mockup.

## Take it further

- **Roles** — "managers can edit any deal, reps only their own."
- **Imports** — attach a CSV and ask the Builder to load it as starting data.
- **Weekly digest** — pair it with an Aura Agent that emails a summary every Monday.
- **Filters and search** — ask for date ranges and per-person views.

![From a prompt to a finished app](/images/use-cases/prompt-to-app.jpg)

## Tips

- Sketch the three numbers your team checks daily and put those on the home page first.
- Internal tools don't need to be pretty on day one — ship, then polish.
