Agencies, accountants and freelancers lose hours to "can you resend that file?" emails. A client portal ends that.

![A glass client portal](/images/use-cases/portal.jpg)

## What to ask for

> Build a client portal for my design studio. Clients sign in and see their projects, each with status, files they can download, invoices, and a message thread. I need an admin side where I create projects and upload files.

The Builder creates sign-in, the client dashboard, file storage and your admin view. Each client only sees their own projects.

## Take it further

- **Approvals** — "clients can approve or request changes on a deliverable."
- **Notifications** — email the client when you upload something new.
- **Branding** — your logo, your domain, your colors: it should feel like your studio, not a tool.
- **Professional email** — Elite members get free mailboxes on their domain, so portal emails come from you@yourstudio.com.

![From a prompt to a finished app](/images/use-cases/prompt-to-app.jpg)

## Tips

- Start with one friendly client and expand once it feels right.
- Keep the first version to three things: files, status, messages. Add the rest later.
