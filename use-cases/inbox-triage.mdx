**You'll use:** [Work Mode](/chat/work-mode) with Gmail or Outlook.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your email">Turn on Work Mode and connect your account when asked.</Step>
  <Step title="Ask for a triage">*"Group today's unread email into urgent, needs a reply and FYI. Summarize each in one line."*</Step>
  <Step title="Draft replies">*"Draft polite replies to the urgent ones. Don't send yet."*</Step>
  <Step title="Make it daily">Turn it into an [Agent](/build/agents) that runs every morning.</Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will Aura send emails on its own?">
    No. Ask for drafts, and agents wait for approval before sending.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which email services work?">
    Gmail and Outlook through Work Mode.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How can I try inbox triage without sending replies?">
    Start with a read-only summary or explicitly request draft-only replies. Inspect the suggested actions before permitting live changes. [Agent trust settings](/agents/trust) help control recurring work.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
