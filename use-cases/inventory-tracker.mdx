Shops, makers and warehouses all need the same thing: a simple system that answers "do we have it?" instantly.

![Glass shelves with stock](/images/use-cases/inventory.jpg)

## What to ask for

> Build an inventory tracker for my candle business. Products with name, SKU, photo, quantity and price. A page to add stock when a batch is made, a page to log a sale that reduces stock, and a low-stock warning list.

The Builder creates the product database, the forms and the low-stock logic. Ask for barcode-style SKUs or categories if you need them.

## Take it further

- **Suppliers and reorder lists** — "when stock drops below 10, add it to a reorder page."
- **Sales history** — charts of what sells best each month.
- **Multiple locations** — track stock per shop or shelf.
- **Team access** — staff can log sales, only you can edit products.

![From a prompt to a finished app](/images/use-cases/prompt-to-app.jpg)

## Tips

- Attach your current spreadsheet — the Builder can turn it into your starting product list.
- Start with quantity tracking only; add costs and margins once it's habit.
