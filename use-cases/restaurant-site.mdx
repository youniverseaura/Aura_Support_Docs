A restaurant site needs to do three things well: show the menu, show the location, and make booking a table effortless.

![A glass restaurant storefront](/images/use-cases/restaurant.jpg)

## What to ask for

> Build a website for an Italian restaurant called Luna. Warm, elegant design. Pages: home with hero photo, full menu with prices, about, and a reservations page where guests pick a date, time and party size.

The Builder creates every page and stores reservations so staff can see them. Add your real menu in the prompt or attach it as a file — the Builder turns it into a proper menu page.

## Take it further

- **Photo gallery** — attach your best dishes and ask for a gallery section.
- **Map and directions** — ask for an embedded map with your address.
- **Daily specials** — ask for a section you can edit without rebuilding.
- **Online ordering** — add a simple order-ahead form with pickup times.

![From a prompt to a finished app](/images/use-cases/prompt-to-app.jpg)

## Tips

- Real photos beat stock every time. Attach them to your prompt.
- Ask for a mobile-first design — most guests will view it on a phone.
