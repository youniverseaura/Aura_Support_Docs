<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the store">*"An online store for handmade candles and ceramics with product pages, a cart, checkout and an admin page to add products."*</Step>
  <Step title="Connect payments">Follow **Setup & keys → Needs** to add your payment account.</Step>
  <Step title="Add your domain">Your first domain on a project is free. See [Custom domains](/builder/domains).</Step>
  <Step title="Publish">Go live and let Aura set up Google for you.</Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which plan do I need?">
    Elite includes the AI App Builder.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I take payments?">
    Follow Setup & keys → Needs to add your payment account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What should I test before accepting real orders?">
    Walk through product selection, cart, checkout and confirmation. Check that orders are saved and any payment notifications are verified using [webhook tests](/builder/setup-keys). A finished screen alone is not a verified checkout.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
