One event, one link. Guests get every detail and you get a live guest list — no spreadsheet wrangling.

![A glass event invitation](/images/use-cases/event.jpg)

## What to ask for

> Build a wedding website. Home page with our story and photos, a schedule page, a travel page with hotel recommendations, and an RSVP form asking for names, plus-ones, meal choice and song requests. Store responses so we can see them.

The Builder creates the pages, the RSVP form and a private responses view for you.

## Take it further

- **Deadline logic** — "close RSVPs on May 1st and show a message after that."
- **Email confirmations** — guests get a thank-you email after responding.
- **Photo upload** — a shared gallery guests can add to after the event.
- **Password** — keep details private with a simple passcode page.

![From a prompt to a finished app](/images/use-cases/prompt-to-app.jpg)

## Tips

- Works for conferences and product launches too — swap RSVP for ticket registration.
- Publish early; guests will visit on phones, so check the mobile preview.
