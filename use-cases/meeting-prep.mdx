## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What do I need connected?">
    Your calendar and email through Work Mode.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it run automatically?">
    Yes, as an Agent before each meeting on Pro or Elite.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What should I include in a meeting-preparation request?">
    Name the calendar and email account, the date range and the desired briefing format. Specify the deadline and where the output should go. Check a first briefing before making it recurring.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
