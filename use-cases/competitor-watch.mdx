**You'll use:** [live web search](/chat/live-search) and an [Agent](/build/agents).

<Steps>
  <Step title="List your competitors">Name them, or let Aura find them: *"Who are the top five bakeries within 10 miles of York, PA?"*</Step>
  <Step title="Define what matters">Prices, new products, reviews, promotions.</Step>
  <Step title="Schedule a weekly brief">*"Every Monday, check these five and email me what changed."*</Step>
</Steps>
