Before the product exists, the landing page can. Test the idea, collect emails, take pre-orders.

![A glass product landing page](/images/use-cases/landing.jpg)

## What to ask for

> Build a landing page for a smart plant sensor. Bold hero with the product photo and a pre-order button, a how-it-works section with three steps, a comparison table, FAQ, and an email signup for launch updates.

The Builder creates the full page with working signup and checkout. Iterate on the copy by chatting — "make the headline punchier" is a valid edit.

## Take it further

- **A/B the headline** — duplicate the project and test two versions.
- **Analytics and SEO** — run the SEO scan so search engines index it properly.
- **Countdown** — "add a launch countdown timer above the fold."
- **Testimonials** — add a quotes section as soon as you have beta users.

![From a prompt to a finished app](/images/use-cases/prompt-to-app.jpg)

## Tips

- One page, one job: every section should push toward the same button.
- Attach the product photo in your first prompt — it anchors the whole design.
