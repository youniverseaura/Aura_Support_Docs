## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which plan do these need?">
    It depends: Work Mode needs Essential or higher, Agents need Pro or Elite and the AI Builder needs Elite.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I adapt a walkthrough?">
    Yes. Swap in your own apps, files and goals.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I adapt a use-case example to my own business?">
    Yes. Replace the example's audience, workflow, fields and output with your actual needs. Start with one complete task or app flow, then expand after checking the result. Examples are a starting brief, not a promise that every integration is already configured.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
