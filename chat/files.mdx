Tap the paperclip or drag files into the composer. Aura reads PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, slides and images.

## Ideas to try

- *"Summarize this contract and flag anything unusual."*
- *"What's in this photo, and what's the brand?"*
- *"Compare these two proposals in a table."*

## Analyze data

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/spreadsheet.jpg" alt="Spreadsheet grid rising into 3D charts" />
</Frame>

Upload a spreadsheet and ask for totals, trends or charts. Ask for the result as a new file and Aura keeps the original format.

<Note>Files attached to a message belong to that chat. To reuse files across chats, add them to a [Project's knowledge base](/chat/projects).</Note>
