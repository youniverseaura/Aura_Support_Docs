When your question depends on current facts — prices, news, weather, scores, schedules — Aura searches the web in that same turn. It doesn't reuse old searches for time-sensitive questions.

## Sources you can check

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/citations.jpg" alt="Answer card linked to three numbered web pages" />
</Frame>

Every searched answer lists its sources. Tap a number to open the page it came from.

<Tip>If Aura says it needs to check something, it searches before answering — it won't promise a lookup and skip it.</Tip>
