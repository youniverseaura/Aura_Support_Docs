**Unlimited** is the everyday mode. Ask as much as you want — messages don't use credits.

## What it does

- Runs one fast, capable model with **Efficient** reasoning.
- Is **always** protected by Zero Data Retention. There's no Fast Mode here, so your prompts are never stored or used for training.
- Searches the live web when facts may have changed and shows sources.
- Supports files, images and [voice](/chat/voice) — a realistic AI voice can read any answer aloud.

## Best for

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/composer.jpg" alt="Glass chat composer with a send button" />
</Frame>

- Writing and rewriting: emails, posts, replies.
- Explaining things and answering questions.
- Summarizing articles and short documents.
- Brainstorming and quick research.

## Good to know

<Note>To keep things fast for everyone, people with very heavy usage may be temporarily slowed down. You're never cut off.</Note>

- Unlimited is available in Quick Chat for every signed-in plan. It isn't offered inside Project Chat.
- For high-stakes questions, switch to [Orchestration](/chat/orchestration).

## How to use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the mode picker">Tap the mode name above the composer.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose Unlimited">Pick the **Unlimited** tab.</Step>
  <Step title="Ask away">Type or speak your question.</Step>
</Steps>
