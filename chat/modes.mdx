Open the mode picker above the composer and choose **Unlimited**, **Orchestration** or **Frontier**. A short card under the tabs reminds you what the selected mode is best for.

## Side-by-side

| | Unlimited | Orchestration | Frontier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Models** | One fast model | ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Grok together | One model you choose |
| **Reasoning** | Efficient | Medium on all four | Low or Efficient |
| **Credits** | Uncapped | About 20 credits per message | Depends on model and level |
| **Zero Data Retention** | Always | Yes, unless Fast Mode is on | Yes, unless Fast Mode is on |
| **Fast Mode** | No | Optional | Optional |
| **Project chats** | Not available | Available | Available |
| **Voice** | Yes | Yes | Yes |

## Quick decision guide

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/prompting-target.jpg" alt="Glass target with an arrow in the center" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Is it an everyday question?">
    Use **Unlimited** — drafts, summaries, explanations, quick lookups.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Would a wrong answer cost you?">
    Use **Orchestration** — money, health, legal, business or technical decisions where a second, third and fourth opinion matters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do you trust one model for this job?">
    Use **Frontier** — for example Claude for code, Gemini for long documents, or Grok for fast-moving topics.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Examples

| Task | Best mode |
| --- | --- |
| "Rewrite this email to sound friendlier" | Unlimited |
| "Should I lease or buy equipment for my business?" | Orchestration |
| "Review this pull request for bugs" | Frontier with Claude at Efficient |
| "Summarize this 80-page report" | Frontier with Gemini |
| "What's the weather in Denver this weekend?" | Unlimited (it searches live) |

## Quick Chat and Project Chat

- **Quick Chat** is your everyday chat from the home screen. All three modes are available.
- **Project Chat** happens inside a [Project](/chat/projects) and always has your project files and connected apps available. Unlimited isn't offered there.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which mode is the most accurate?">
    Orchestration. Four models answer independently, challenge each other, and Aura combines the result.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which mode uses the fewest credits?">
    Unlimited — it's uncapped.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where did Balanced and Thinking go?">
    They were replaced by the three modes. For more depth, use Frontier at Efficient or Orchestration.
