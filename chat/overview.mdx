## Why Aura's answers are different

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/four-models.jpg" alt="Four colored spheres beaming light into one prism" />
</Frame>

Aura can ask GPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok the same question and combine the best of each. When facts change — prices, news, weather — it searches the live web and cites sources.
