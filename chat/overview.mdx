Choose **Unlimited**, **Orchestration** or **Frontier** for answers, research and file analysis. All three support realistic AI voice. When you want Aura to act inside connected apps, open the dedicated [Work Mode guide](/chat/work-mode). Work Mode is an execution capability, not a fourth response mode. Every chat runs in one of **three modes**, and you can switch at any time from the mode picker above the composer.

## The three modes at a glance

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/four-models.jpg" alt="Four colored spheres beaming light into one prism" />
</Frame>

| Mode | Tag in the app | What it does | Pick it when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Unlimited](/chat/unlimited)** | Everyday | One fast model, uncapped, always protected by Zero Data Retention. | You want quick answers all day without counting messages. |
| **[Orchestration](/chat/orchestration)** | Highest accuracy | Four frontier models answer, debate, and are combined into one vetted answer. | Being wrong would be costly. |
| **[Frontier](/chat/frontier)** | Your pick | One frontier model you choose — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Grok — at Low or Efficient reasoning. | You prefer one model's strengths. |

<Tip>Every mode supports [voice](/chat/voice): a realistic AI voice can speak any answer, and you can talk back hands-free.</Tip>

## Things every mode shares

- **Live web search** with sources whenever facts may have changed. See [Live web search](/chat/live-search).
- **Files and images** you attach. See [Add files](/chat/files).
- **Zero Data Retention** by default — models don't store or train on your prompts. The only exception is the optional Fast Mode in Orchestration and Frontier, which is clearly marked.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which mode should I start with?">
    Unlimited for everyday questions. Switch to Orchestration for important decisions, or Frontier when you want a specific model.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I switch modes mid-conversation?">
    Yes. Change the mode in the picker and your next message uses it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Aura search the web?">
    Yes, in every mode. When facts change, it searches live and cites sources.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Aura act inside my apps?">
    Yes, with Work Mode on Essential, Pro and Elite.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I ask Aura to draft without sending?">
    Say **draft only — do not send or post**. A response mode is not a read-only permission setting; an explicit app-action request through [Work Mode](/chat/work-mode) can change the connected account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
