The Aura AI App Builder turns a description into a working app — with a database, sign-in, payments and email — and publishes it to the web.

## What you can build

<Frame>
  <img
    src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/builder-categories.jpg"

Online stores, booking apps, dashboards, community and church apps, internal tools — and 3D games you can play or sell in weeks, not months.

## The workspace

Chat on the left; on the right, tabs for **Preview, History, Files, SEO, Leads, Security, Performance** and **Settings**.

<Note>The AI App Builder connects to services through built-in features, your own API keys and MCP servers — not the 1,500+ apps used by Aura chat.</Note>
