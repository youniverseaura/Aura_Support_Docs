The Aura AI App Builder turns a description into a working app — with a database, sign-in, payments and email — hosted on Google Cloud.

## Build your first app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a New App">Open **AI Builder** and describe what you want, or pick a category like Website, Online store, Booking or Game.</Step>
  <Step title="Chat to refine it">Ask for changes in plain words. Watch the live preview update.</Step>
  <Step title="Add keys and services">The **Setup & keys** panel tells you exactly which accounts are needed and where to find each key.</Step>
  <Step title="Publish">Publish to a free Aura address, or connect or buy your own domain.</Step>
</Steps>

## What's included

- Database, file storage and user accounts
- Built-in AI, payments and transactional email
- Custom domains and domain email
- Automatic SEO, security and performance scans
- Collaboration with viewer, editor and admin roles
- Restore any earlier version

<Note>The AI App Builder connects to services through built-in features, your own API keys and MCP servers — not the 1,500+ apps used by Aura chat.</Note>

<Info>The AI App Builder is included with Elite.</Info>
