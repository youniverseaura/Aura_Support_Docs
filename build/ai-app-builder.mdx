## The workspace

Chat on the left; on the right, tabs for **Preview, History, Files, SEO, Leads, Security, Performance** and **Settings**. Settings has its own menu: **Usage & billing, Data, Logs, App users, Storage, Scheduled tasks, In-app AI, Domain, Setup** and **Code & integrations**.

<Note>The AI App Builder connects to services through built-in features, your own API keys and MCP servers — not the 1,500+ apps used by Aura chat.</Note>

<Info>The AI App Builder is included with Elite.</Info>
