## Examples

- Every morning, summarize industry news and email it to me.
- When a new lead arrives, research the company and add notes to my CRM.
- Every Friday, build a sales report in Google Sheets.

## How they work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the job">Write what the agent should do in plain language.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose a trigger">Press Run, set a schedule, hand it a job from a chat, or let a connected app, a web address or its own quiet check-ins start it.</Step>
  <Step title="Review and approve">Risky actions — sending, deleting, spending — ask for your approval first.</Step>
  <Step title="Watch it run">Every run shows its steps, files and results.</Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which plans include Agents?">
    Pro and Elite.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What can start an agent?">
    Any of these, and you can turn on as many as you like:

    - **When you press Run** — always available.
    - **On a schedule** — at the times you pick.
    - **From a chat** — you or Aura can hand it a job mid-conversation.
    - **When a connected app does something** — a new email, order or row, for example.
    - **From a web address** — anything that can send a request to its private link.
    - **Quiet check-ins between runs** — it takes a short look on its own and only acts if something's worth it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I see what an agent did?">
    Yes. Every run shows its steps, files and results.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is an Agent the same as an app made with AI Builder?">
    No. Agents carry out jobs in connected services on a trigger or schedule. The [Aura AI App Builder](/build/ai-app-builder) creates an app with its own screens, logic and data. Choose based on whether you need a recurring task or a product people use.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
