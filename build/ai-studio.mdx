AI Studio is Aura's creative production workspace. Its five main tabs are **Imagine**, **Music**, **Speech**, **Transcribe** and **Media**. Use them together to move from an idea to artwork, moving pictures, a soundtrack, narration and finished files.

## Every part of the workspace

| Tab | Features |
| --- | --- |
| Imagine | Image generation, references, masking/inpainting, video generation, compatible start/end frames and image/video/audio references, portrait lip sync, Explore, History and Remix. |
| Music | New songs, Auto/Custom/Instrumental lyrics, duration and music-version selection, video scoring, curated/custom finetunes, song analysis, targeted edits, extension, restyling, exact-time insertion and Saved Edits. |
| Speech | Voice discovery and previews, language/accent/category/gender filters, model and delivery controls, expressive scripts, output formats and searchable narration history. |
| Transcribe | Audio/video upload, automatic or manual language, speaker diarization, audio-event tagging, copying and downloading transcripts. |
| Media | Type/provider filters, previews, references, downloads, rename/delete, lyrics extraction/download and song editing. |

## Combine the tools

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the visual">
    Make an image in Imagine and use it as a supported video reference, or prepare a portrait for Lip sync.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add voice or music">
    Create narration in Speech, compose a track in Music, or score an uploaded video with Music's Video mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine the result">
    Remix visual work or target music sections. Use Saved Edits when several song changes belong together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and keep the files">
    Play and inspect the finished output, then download it or return through the workspace's history and Media library.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Access, credits and privacy

AI Studio generation/editing controls require **Pro or Elite**. Free and Essential members can explore preview-only controls; browsing does not unlock generation. Free members can separately generate **2 images per rolling hour in chat**.

Choose a model and supported settings, then read the displayed credit estimate. Image/video models, duration, resolution, variations, narration length, music generation, edits and training can affect usage. Do not assume all operations have one flat price.

Imagine and Music display **Non-ZDR** notices. Speech and Transcribe have their own privacy badges. A ZDR setting in chat does not turn a different creative service into a ZDR service. Avoid sensitive prompts and private references in Non-ZDR operations. See [plans](/account/plans) and [privacy](/account/privacy).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Imagine only for images?">
    No. Imagine includes Image, Video and Lip sync; its settings depend on the selected mode and model.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit music after generation?">
    Yes. The song editor supports section targeting, precise insertion, extending and restyling, plus Saved Edits applied together.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Transcribe the same as Speech?">
    No. Speech turns text into audio; Transcribe turns an uploaded recording into text.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where is Aura Agents?">
    [Aura Agents](/build/agents) has its own top-level docs tab. It handles recurring or event-driven work, not Studio production settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to review privacy separately when combining creative tools?">
    Yes. Each operation has its own privacy badge and credit estimate. A ZDR chat setting or Speech badge does not cover a later image, music or lip-sync operation. Review the badge before submitting private references.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
