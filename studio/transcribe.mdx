Open **AI Studio → Transcribe** to convert an audio or video recording into text. This is a file-based transcription workspace, not the live voice-chat microphone.

## Upload and configure
<Steps>
<Step title="Choose the recording">Drop an audio/video file into the upload area or select it from your device. The upload ceiling is 1 GB.</Step>
<Step title="Choose language">Use Auto-detect or select the recording's language when you know it.</Step>
<Step title="Choose speaker and event options">Speaker diarization separates different speakers. Audio event tagging includes supported non-speech events such as laughter or music.</Step>
<Step title="Transcribe and inspect">Run transcription, then review the text against the recording before using it as a final record.</Step>
</Steps>

## What the options do
| Option | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| Auto-detect | You are unsure of the spoken language. |
| Manual language | You know the language and want to specify it. |
| Speaker diarization | A meeting, interview or recording contains multiple speakers. |
| Audio event tagging | Non-speech sounds matter to the transcript. |

Speaker labels distinguish voices; they do not reliably identify a person's real name. Correct names, technical terms and numbers during review.

## Copy or download the transcript

Use **Copy transcript** to copy the result, or **Download transcript** to save a `.txt` file with timestamps and speaker labels. Review the transcription before treating those labels and words as final.

## Better source material
Use a clear recording with minimal background noise. Speaker overlap, distant microphones and music can make transcription harder. If the result is important, listen and check it instead of relying on an unreviewed transcript.
