## Finding your way around

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/workspace-overview.jpg" alt="Glass panels showing a sidebar, chat area and message bar" />
</Frame>

- **Sidebar** — recent chats, [Projects](/chat/projects) and every part above. Settings, billing and connected apps live at the bottom.
- **Chat** — answers appear here with their sources, files and any steps Aura took in your apps.
- **Composer** — the message bar. Pick a [chat mode](/chat/modes), attach files, open the **\+** menu for Work Mode and Browser Tool, or tap the microphone for [Voice mode](/chat/voice).
