Aura has three main areas: the **sidebar**, the **chat** and the **composer**.

## The sidebar

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/workspace-sidebar.jpg" alt="Glass sidebar with folder, chat and palette icons" />
</Frame>

Open your recent chats, [Projects](/chat/projects), [Agents](/build/agents), [AI Studio](/build/ai-studio) and the [AI Builder](/build/ai-app-builder). Settings, billing and connected apps live at the bottom.

## The chat

Answers appear here with their sources, files and any steps Aura took in your apps.

## The composer

The message bar at the bottom. From it you can:

- Pick a [chat mode](/chat/modes)
- Attach files with the paperclip
- Open the **+** menu for Work Mode and Browser Tool
- Tap the microphone for [Voice mode](/chat/voice)

<Tip>Your credit balance and a short chat ID appear under the composer. Share the ID with support for faster help.</Tip>
