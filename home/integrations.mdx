## Connecting an app

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/connect.jpg" alt="Two glass puzzle pieces clicking together" />
</Frame>

Just ask for the task. If the app isn't connected yet, Aura shows a connect button — sign in with your own account. Disconnect anytime under **Settings → Connected apps**.
