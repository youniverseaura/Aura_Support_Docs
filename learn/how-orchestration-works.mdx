Every AI model has blind spots. **Orchestration** asks GPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok the same question independently, then Aura compares their answers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Independent answers">Each model answers without seeing the others.</Step>
  <Step title="Comparison">Aura finds where they agree, where they disagree and why.</Step>
  <Step title="One answer">You get a single combined answer — the smartest answer from every angle.</Step>
</Steps>
