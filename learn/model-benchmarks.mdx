## Why Aura combines models

No single model wins everything. Opus leads on design and code, GPT-6 Astra on agentic work, Gemini 3.8 Flash on speed with native audio and video, Grok on live information. [Orchestration](/learn/how-orchestration-works) asks four of them and compares, so you get each model's strengths and catch their blind spots.
