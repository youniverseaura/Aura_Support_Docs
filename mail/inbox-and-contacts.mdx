## Your inbox

The left rail holds your folders: **Inbox, Starred, Drafts, Sent, Archive, Junk and Trash**. Search sits at the top of the message list.

### Smart categories

Chips above the list split your inbox Apple Mail-style:

| Category | What lands there |
| --- | --- |
| Primary | Real people writing to you |
| Transactions | Receipts, orders and shipping |
| Updates | Notifications and account messages |
| Promotions | Newsletters and offers |
| All Mail | Everything together |

Conversations are grouped into threads with a count, unread mail shows a blue dot, and **Select** lets you act on many messages at once.

## Contacts that fill themselves in

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/mail-contacts.jpg" alt="A glass contact card with details flowing in" />
</Frame>

Switch to **Contacts** at the top of the Mail window. When someone new emails you, Aura quietly looks them up on the public web and fills in the blanks:

- **Photo** — from LinkedIn, Gravatar, GitHub or X, or the company's own icon
- **Job title and company**
- **Headline, location and LinkedIn profile**

Open any contact and press **Find details on LinkedIn** to run the lookup again.

<Tip>
  Aura never overwrites what you typed. Automatic details only fill empty fields.
</Tip>

### More contact features

- **My Card** at the top holds your own details.
- **Import** an address book exported from Gmail, iCloud, Outlook or any vCard/CSV file — people you already have are kept.
- Add phones, addresses, birthdays, notes and social links by hand.
