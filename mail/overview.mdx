## At A Glance

Aura Mail is a full email inbox that lives inside Aura. Your address uses **your own domain** — like `hello@yourbusiness.com` — instead of a free webmail address, so every message you send looks professional.

Open it from the **Mail** entry in the sidebar. Everything — inbox, contacts, compose and settings — opens in one window.

## Free professional mail with Elite

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/mail-elite.jpg" alt="A glass crown badge beside a glass mailbox" />
</Frame>

Every **Elite** member gets a professional mailbox at no extra cost:

- **Your first mailbox is included** with 5 GB of storage — no extra credits per month.
- **Use a domain from the AI Builder.** Buy or connect a domain for your app, then create addresses on it. DNS for sending and receiving is set up for you.
- **Need more?** Extra mailboxes and larger storage are available and are paid with credits. Aura shows the monthly cost before you confirm.

<Info>
  Mail is part of Elite. On other plans the Mail window still opens so you can see it, but everything inside is locked until you upgrade.
</Info>
