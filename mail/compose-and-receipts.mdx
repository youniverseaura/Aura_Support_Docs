## Writing a message

Press **New** to open the composer. It can be minimised or expanded to full screen.

- **Rich formatting** — fonts, sizes, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, text colour, alignment, lists, indents, quotes and links
- **Cc/Bcc** and contact suggestions as you type
- **Attachments** with the paperclip
- **Save draft** to finish later

### Let AI write it

The AI row under the message helps you write:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Write it for me | Drafts the whole email from a short note |
| Enhance | Improves clarity and flow |
| Shorten | Cuts it down to the essentials |
| Make formal | Switches to a professional tone |

### Signatures

In **Settings → Signatures**, create one or more signatures with rich text or your own HTML. In **Composing**, choose whether your signature is added to new messages, replies and forwards.

## Read receipts

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/mail-receipts.jpg" alt="A glass envelope opening with an eye and a double check" />
</Frame>

Turn on **Receipt** before you send and Aura tells you when the message is opened:

- The sent message shows when it was **first opened**, when it was **last opened**, and **how many times**.
- You get an alert when it's read, which takes you straight to the message.
- To ask for receipts on every new message, turn on **Settings → Composing → Ask for a read receipt by default**. You can still switch it off per message.

<Note>
  Read receipts rely on the recipient's mail app loading images. Some people block images, so a message can be read without a receipt.
</Note>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I stop senders tracking when I open their mail?">
    Yes. In **Settings → Reading**, turn off loading remote images. Images stay hidden until you choose to show them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does AI writing cost credits?">
    AI writing uses a small amount of credits, like other AI features.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a read receipt prove someone read every word?">
    No. A receipt is an open signal, not proof of attention or understanding. Do not use it as the only confirmation of an important agreement; ask for a reply when confirmation matters.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
