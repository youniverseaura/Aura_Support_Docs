## Trust score

Every incoming message is checked for impersonation and social-engineering tricks. Suspicious mail shows a banner with:

- A **trust score from 0 to 100**
- The **specific warning signs** Aura found — like a look-alike sender domain, urgent pressure or a request for passwords or payment

The check runs alongside spam filtering, so dangerous mail is flagged before you click anything.

## Blocked senders

<Frame>
  <img src="https://cdn.youniverse.ai/blog-hero/docs/mail-inbox.jpg" alt="Glass inbox window" />
</Frame>

In **Settings → Blocked**, see and remove addresses and domains you've blocked. Mail from them never reaches your inbox.

## Safer reading

- Scripts and embedded frames are removed from every message.
- Turn off remote images in **Settings → Reading** to stop senders knowing when you open their mail.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What should I do with a low trust score?">
    Don't click links or reply. Block the sender or move the message to Junk.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my mail used to train AI?">
    No. See [Privacy](/account/privacy).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a high trust score guarantee an email is genuine?">
    No. Use the score and warnings as evidence, not a guarantee. Check the sender and request before sharing sensitive information or following payment instructions. Block the sender or domain when appropriate.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
