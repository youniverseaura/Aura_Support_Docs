<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [aura.youniverse.ai](https://aura.youniverse.ai) and sign in with Apple or with your email (we send you a sign-in link). Aura also runs as an app on iPhone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your first question">
    Type anything into the composer and press Enter. Aura searches the live web when your question needs current information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a mode">
    Switch between fast answers, deeper thinking and multi-model orchestration. See [Chat modes](/chat/modes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach files or talk instead">
    Drop in PDFs, documents, spreadsheets or images, or tap the microphone to start [Voice mode](/chat/voice).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your apps">
    Turn on [Work Mode](/chat/work-mode) from the **+** menu and connect Gmail, Google Drive, Slack and more.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I sign in?">
    With Apple, or with your email and a sign-in link.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a mobile app?">
    Yes, on iPhone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I start with a document instead of an empty question?">
    Yes. Attach a PDF, document, spreadsheet or image and describe what you need from it. Specify a summary, comparison, table or finished file. See [Add files](/chat/files).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
