<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [aura.youniverse.ai](https://aura.youniverse.ai) and sign in with Google, Apple or email. Aura also runs as an app on iPhone and Android.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your first question">
    Type anything into the composer at the bottom of the screen and press Enter. Aura searches the live web when your question needs current information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a mode">
    Use the mode picker to switch between fast answers, deeper thinking and multi-model orchestration. See [Chat modes](/chat/modes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach files or talk instead">
    Drop in PDFs, documents, spreadsheets or images, or tap the microphone to start [Voice mode](/chat/voice).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your apps">
    Turn on [Work Mode](/chat/work-mode) from the **+** menu and connect Gmail, Google Drive, Slack and more so Aura can act for you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Ask Aura itself: "What can you do?" It knows its own features and plans.</Tip>
