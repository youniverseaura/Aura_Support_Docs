Aura is built by **Youniverse.AI**, based in York County, Pennsylvania.

## Our story

Youniverse.AI began in 2020 as **iTelligent Solutions**, a subscription-based creative agency. Working on websites, branding and SEO for clients showed how fragmented AI tools had become, so the company relaunched as Youniverse.AI in 2024 to build one assistant that brings the best models together.

## Founder

**Matthias Hamm** is the founder and CEO — a software engineer, web developer and SEO specialist.
